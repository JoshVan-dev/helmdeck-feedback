Helmdeck vs Pinokio

They solve different problems, and plenty of people use both. Pinokio is an installer for ready-made open-source AI apps. Helmdeck is the place you run and watch everything, including the bots and scripts you build yourself.

The short version

Pick Pinokio if you mainly want to try popular AI apps (image, video or music generators and chatbots) without touching a terminal. It's free and has a large catalog of one-click installs.

Pick Helmdeck if you build your own things with AI, like trading or Discord bots, scrapers, agents or game tools, and you're tired of hunting through terminals to see what's running. Helmdeck also installs popular AI apps, finds the ones you already have, and keeps all of it on one screen.

Where Helmdeck is different

It's built for the things you make

Point Helmdeck at any folder or command and it becomes a tile. Two lines of code from your build put a live number on that tile: today's P/L, a progress bar, or a "needs you" flag when it's waiting on a decision.

It keeps things running

Turn on "Restart if it stops" and a crashed bot is back in seconds. Builds can start with your PC or run on a schedule, and with phone alerts you hear about problems while you're out.

It ships with real starter builds

Browse opens with official starter builds, like a daily AI news digest, chat with your files, a price tracker and an uptime monitor. Press Make it and they're running.
